What a tax invoice must show

  • The words "Tax invoice"
  • Your business name and ABN
  • Date of issue
  • Description, quantity and price of what you sold
  • GST payable (or "Total price includes GST")
  • Buyer's identity or ABN if the sale is $1,000 or more

Source: ATO — Tax invoices.
