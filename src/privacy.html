Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your invoice data stays on your device. The calculators and invoice generator run entirely in your browser. Details you enter are not sent to us; the invoice generator may save a draft in your browser's local storage so you don't lose work. Clear it any time with the "Clear saved data" button or your browser settings.

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security purposes (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Analytics and advertising. If enabled, we may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. Plausible) or Google Analytics, and Google AdSense, which may set cookies. These are only loaded when configured, and this policy will apply to them.

Payments and affiliate links. Purchases are processed by a third-party provider (e.g. Stripe or Gumroad) under their privacy policy. Affiliate links may let the partner know you came from this site.

We handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply. Questions: see the contact in the footer.
