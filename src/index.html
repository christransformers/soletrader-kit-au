What must be on an Australian tax invoice?

Per the ATO, a tax invoice for a sale under $1,000 must let the buyer determine: that it is a tax invoice, the seller's identity, the seller's ABN, the issue date, a description of items (with quantity and price), the GST payable, and the extent to which each sale is taxable. For sales of $1,000 or more it must also show the buyer's identity or ABN. If you're not registered for GST, issue an "Invoice", not a "Tax invoice" (business.gov.au).
